About G's

A neighbourhood
burger kitchen.
Not a chain.

G's Gourmet Burgers has been a fixture on the corner of Wellington Street since well before Luton's burger boom. We're independent. We're Halal. We're run by people who live here.

Our promise is the one printed on our own wall: personalised burgers, steaks & much more. Walk in, tell us what you want, watch us build it. Single, double, triple. Beef, chicken, lamb, veggie. Sweet potato fries or hand-cut chips. Skewers, platters, wraps if you're on the move. Milkshakes thick enough to need a spoon.

No microwave reheats. No frozen patties from a multinational. Just a kitchen on Wellington Street, cooking each plate when the order lands — the way gourmet should mean something rather than just sit on a sign.
